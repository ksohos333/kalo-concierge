# Kalo Concierge

**A WhatsApp or Viber assistant for small Greek hotels, built from conversations
with hotel owners.**

Live site: **[kaloconcierge.com](https://kaloconcierge.com)** · English · Ελληνικά

---

## The problem

I started by researching how small hotels in Greece handle guest messages and
calls — before guests arrive, and during their stay. The same three things came
up in every conversation:

- **The same guest questions repeat.** What time can guests arrive, is there
  breakfast, where can they park. Answering them takes up your day.
- **Messages and calls arrive at night**, or when you are busy with something
  else.
- **Guests write in many languages**, so replying properly takes extra time on
  top of the answering itself.

For a hotel with no reception desk, the owner is the front desk — and a
recurring question is the same thing as a lost booking opportunity.

## What I am building

A small pilot: an assistant on the hotel's own **WhatsApp or Viber** number that

- **answers common questions automatically, in the guest's own language;**
- **sends anything about bookings straight to the owner**, so the hotel stays in
  control;
- **only uses information the hotel has given it**, and never invents prices,
  availability or policies.

The third point is the one that decides whether this is useful at all. A guest
who is told "the rate is €90" when it is actually €70 has been told a lie by
your business, and a guest who is told the pool is open when it is closed will
write a review. The assistant is constrained to the hotel's own information.

## How it started

This is not a product spec that came first. It is a small pilot built from
conversations with hotel owners, and the questions in this README are the ones
those conversations raised.

If the problem above sounds familiar, a 15-minute call is enough. You explain
how things work at your hotel, and you decide whether a pilot makes sense for
you. No obligation.

## Privacy

Short version: **I am the only person who handles your details.** Your name,
your hotel, and notes from our conversations.

- I keep them for **12 months** after we last spoke, then delete them.
- I **do not record calls** unless you agree first.
- For a pilot hotel, guest messages are used **only to answer your guests**.
- To work, they pass through the messaging app (WhatsApp or Viber) and the AI
  service that drafts the replies. **I will tell you in writing which services
  are involved before anything goes live.**
- I do not sell or share anything, and the site uses **no cookies or tracking**.
- You can ask me at any time to see, correct or delete what I hold, or
  contact the [Hellenic Data Protection Authority](https://www.dpa.gr).

**Opting out:** reply to any message with "unsubscribe" and I will not write
to you again. No reason needed.
